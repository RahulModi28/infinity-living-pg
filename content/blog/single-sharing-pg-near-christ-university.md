## What "single sharing" means

In Bengaluru PG listings, "single sharing" means a room with one bed — you don't share it with anyone. "Double sharing" is two beds in one room, "triple sharing" three, and so on. The common areas — dining hall, lounge, gym — are shared either way.

The word "sharing" confuses a lot of first-time renters, and some listings use it loosely. Ask how many beds are in the room, not just which category it's in.

## Who a single room suits

A single room costs ₹4,000 a month more than a bed in a double. That's ₹48,000 across a 12-month stay, so it's worth being honest about whether you need it. It usually earns its cost if you:

- **Sleep lightly, or keep different hours** from most people — late-night study, early lectures, a part-time job.
- **Study in your room** rather than a library, and need quiet during exam and submission weeks.
- **Take calls or online classes** from your room.
- **Have lived alone before** and know a roommate would wear on you.

Double sharing tends to suit you better if you're new to the city and would like someone to settle in with, or if the ₹4,000 a month matters more than the privacy.

## What the rent should include — anywhere

The headline rent of a single room tells you little on its own. Before comparing two PGs, ask each one:

1. **Is electricity included, or billed from a meter?** In a single room there's no one to split the bill with.
2. **How many meals a day, and on which days?** Many PGs serve two meals, or cut back on weekends.
3. **Are Wi-Fi, housekeeping and laundry in the rent, or extra?**
4. **What's the deposit, and how do you get it back?**
5. **Is there a lock-in or minimum stay?**

At Infinity Space the answers are: electricity is included; four meals a day Monday to Friday, breakfast and lunch on Saturday, a full day on Sunday; Wi-Fi, housekeeping and laundry are included; the deposit is two months' rent, adjusted against April and May; and the minimum stay is 12 months.
