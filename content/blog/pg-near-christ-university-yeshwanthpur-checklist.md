Most PG listings near the Yeshwanthpur campus look alike: furnished rooms, food, Wi-Fi, a photo of a bed. The differences are in the details that come up after you've paid the deposit. Here's what's worth asking any PG before you book — and how we answer each question at Infinity Space, so you have something to compare against.

## 1. What does the rent actually include?

Ask for a single number that covers everything, then ask what isn't in it. Electricity is the usual surprise — some places bill it separately from a sub-meter, and in a Bengaluru summer that adds up.

**At Infinity Space:** ₹20,000 a month for a single room and ₹16,000 per person for double sharing. That covers the furnished room, electricity, meals, Wi-Fi, housekeeping and laundry.

## 2. How much is the deposit, and how do you get it back?

A deposit you have to chase at the end of the year is a deposit you may not see again. Ask how it's returned, not just how much it is.

**At Infinity Space:** two months' rent — ₹40,000 for single, ₹32,000 for double. It isn't refunded at the end; it's adjusted against your April and May rent, so you pay no rent for those two months.

## 3. Is there a minimum stay?

Many PGs have a lock-in and only mention it at signing. Find out before you visit.

**At Infinity Space:** 12 months — it lines up with the deposit arrangement above.

## 4. How far is it, really?

"Near campus" can mean anything. Ask for the walking distance by road, not a straight line on a map, and walk it once at the time you'd leave for class.

**At Infinity Space:** we're on Andrahalli Main Road, HMT Layout — about 850 m by road from Christ University Yeshwanthpur Campus, roughly a 10 minute walk. A supermarket, a pharmacy and a hospital are all within about 600 m.

## 5. What's the food like, and when is it served?

Ask how many meals a day, whether it's cooked on site, and what happens on weekends — that's where most PG kitchens cut back.

**At Infinity Space:** four meals a day Monday to Friday, cooked on site — breakfast, lunch, evening snacks and dinner. Saturday is breakfast and lunch only. Sunday is back to a full day.

## 6. What are the rules?

Curfew, visitors and guests are the rules that affect daily life most. Ask for them plainly, and ask whether there's any flexibility.

**At Infinity Space:** we're a gents-only PG. Male visitors are allowed. There's an 11 pm curfew, and later nights are possible with a parent's permission.

## 7. Can you visit before you book?

Photos only show what the owner chose to photograph. A PG that won't let you or your parents see the room first is telling you something.

**At Infinity Space:** parents are welcome to visit before you book — we'd rather you saw the place than booked from photos. You can also look through our [photo gallery](/gallery) first.
