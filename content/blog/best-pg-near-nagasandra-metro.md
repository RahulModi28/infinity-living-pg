## What "near Nagasandra" actually means

Nagasandra Metro is on the Green Line in north-west Bengaluru, on the Tumkur Road side of the city. When a listing says "near Nagasandra", it can mean Nagasandra itself or any of the areas around it — HMT Layout, Peenya, 8th Mile or Jalahalli Cross — so check the actual walking distance to the station rather than trusting the area name.

Walk the route once, at the time you'd actually leave in the morning. A PG that is 500 m away on a map can be much further on foot if there's a main road or a railway line in between.

## What a PG near Nagasandra costs — and what the price leaves out

The large coliving chains advertise beds near Nagasandra from about ₹8,000 a month, and local listing sites show a wide spread above and below that. The headline number almost never tells you the full monthly cost. Before comparing two PGs, ask each one:

- **Is electricity included, or billed from a meter?** In summer this can add a noticeable amount to the month.
- **How many meals a day, and on which days?** Many PGs serve two meals, or cut back on weekends.
- **Is laundry included?**
- **How many people share the room?** The cheapest beds are usually in triple or four-sharing rooms.
- **How much is the deposit, and how is it returned?**

A ₹9,000 bed with electricity, laundry and a third meal on top can end up close to a PG that looked much more expensive.

## Gents PG, ladies PG or coliving?

Most people searching around Nagasandra look for one of three things: a gents (boys) PG, a ladies (girls) PG, or a mixed coliving space. They suit different people.

- **Gents-only and ladies-only PGs** are usually what parents prefer for students. The rules on visitors and timings are typically clearer.
- **Coliving spaces** house men and women in the same building, often on separate floors. Many working professionals like the flexibility.

Check which one a listing is before visiting — several listings near Nagasandra are marked "boys, girls and couples" on the same page.

## Big chains or an independent PG?

Around Nagasandra you'll find managed chains such as Stanza Living and Zolo alongside independent, owner-run PGs. Neither is automatically better.

**Chains** give you a standard product: consistent rooms across many locations, a support process, and often the option to move to another of their properties. **Independent PGs** vary a lot more, but the owner is usually on site, you see the exact room you'll live in, and questions get answered by the person who decides.

Whichever you choose, the checks are the same: visit in person, get the rent, deposit and minimum stay in writing, and ask a current resident how the food actually is.

## Before you pay: a quick checklist

1. The all-in monthly rent, in writing
2. Deposit amount and exactly how it comes back
3. Minimum stay or lock-in period
4. Walking time to the metro, and to your college or office
5. Meals per day, including Saturday and Sunday
6. Curfew and visitor rules
7. A visit to see the actual room — not a sample one
